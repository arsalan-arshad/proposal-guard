Proposal Guard: prepare a prompt

Paste the client's job post or contract and your draft. The fixed rules run on this page, then the page builds a ready-to-copy prompt that you can paste into any assistant you choose to get wording suggestions. Optional and experimental.

What leaves your device: nothing. This page has no key field and makes no network requests (its content-security policy forbids them). It builds text and shows it to you. If you copy that text into an assistant, you are the one sending it, and that service's terms apply.

Why not just use Gemini, ChatGPT or Claude directly? You can, and for the wording itself they may do as well or better. We do not claim better writing. What this page adds:

  • The same result every time. The marks come from fixed rules, not from a model, so the same text always gives the same marks, each with the rule name and the exact quoted sentence.
  • Numbers checked by code. Models often miss or invent figures. Here code reads "30 days" against "14 days" and shows which two sentences disagree. Paste a reply back below and code lists any number in it that is in neither of your texts.
  • Private by default. You see the marks without sending a confidential contract anywhere. Only if you choose to, you paste the prompt, or just the sentences that were flagged, into an assistant.
  • A safer prompt. Both texts are put in labelled data blocks after fixed, public instructions that say to treat them as data and not as instructions, not to invent numbers or prices (blanks such as [N] instead), and not to make legal claims.

When to skip this: if you are happy to share your texts and only want something rewritten, paste them into your assistant directly.

Before you paste anywhere: check the terms of that service. For example, Google's terms for unpaid Gemini API use (read 2026-11-15) say submitted content may be used to improve Google products and may be read by human reviewers. On any free service, use invented text or text you are happy to share.

Not legal advice. An assistant can be wrong and can write confidently about things that are not in your documents. Treat every suggestion as a draft to check. Model suggestions are not evaluated and no accuracy is claimed.
