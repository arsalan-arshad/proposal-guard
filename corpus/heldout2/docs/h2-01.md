# Website Redesign: Statement of Work

Prepared for Harbourline Dental Studio by Northfield Web Works.

## 1. Deliverables

1. A five-page marketing website (Home, Services, Team, Pricing, Contact) built on the agreed template.
2. A contact form that sends to one mailbox chosen by the Client.
3. Basic on-page SEO: titles, meta descriptions and alt text for all images on the five pages.

## 2. Timeline

Design mockups are delivered on 14 March. Build is complete on 4 April.

## 3. Review and approval

The Client has 5 business days to review each milestone and reply with approval or written comments. If no reply arrives within 5 business days, the milestone is treated as approved and the next one begins.

## 4. Revisions

Each milestone includes 2 rounds of revisions. A round is one consolidated list of changes. Further rounds, or work outside section 1, are quoted separately at 65 per hour and start only after the Client signs a change order.

## 5. Third-party costs

The Client pays for the domain name, hosting (Meridian Cloud shared plan, about 12 per month) and the stock photo licence directly to those providers. Northfield Web Works will not purchase these on the Client's behalf.
