# Support Chatbot Prototype

Prepared for Orchard Lane Insurance Brokers.

## Scope

I will build a customer support chatbot that answers questions from your FAQ pages and hands over to a human when it is unsure. It will integrate with your website chat widget and give accurate answers across a wide range of topics.

Included:
- Ingestion of your existing FAQ and policy pages
- A hosted prototype using a large language model API
- Vector search over the content
- Usage dashboard, logging and analytics, and similar tooling as needed
- A handover call
