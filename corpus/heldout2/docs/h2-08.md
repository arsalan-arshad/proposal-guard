# CI/CD Pipeline Setup: Scope of Work

**Client:** Tallow Street Games
**Contractor:** Ferris DevOps

## Deliverables
1. A GitHub Actions workflow that runs unit tests, builds a Docker image and pushes it to the Client's container registry on every merge to `main`.
2. A staging deployment on the Client's existing Kubernetes cluster, triggered automatically after step 1.
3. A runbook of no more than 4 pages describing rollback.

## Acceptance
Work is accepted when the pipeline completes green on three consecutive merges. The Client will confirm or report defects within 3 business days of the third run; silence after 3 business days counts as acceptance.

## Changes
Defects against the above are fixed free of charge for 30 days. Requests for new environments, new services or other additions are handled by written change order, billed at 90 per hour with a written estimate before work begins.

## Costs paid by the Client
Cloud compute, container registry storage, GitHub Actions minutes beyond the free tier, and any monitoring licences are billed to the Client's own accounts. Ferris DevOps will not hold any paid subscriptions for this project.

## Fee
2,750, payable 50% at start and 50% at acceptance.
