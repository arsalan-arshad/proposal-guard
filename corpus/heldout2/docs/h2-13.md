# Mobile App MVP: Agreement

Between Rivet Labs ("Developer") and Piper Tutoring ("Client").

**1. Scope.** The Developer will build one React Native app for iOS and Android with: email sign-in, a list of tutors, tutor booking for 30-minute slots, and push reminders 1 hour before a session. Nothing outside this list is in scope.

**2. Milestones.** Milestone A (sign-in and tutor list) by 20 June. Milestone B (booking and reminders) by 18 July.

**3. Review.** The Client shall review each milestone within 5 business days of delivery and either approve it or send a single written list of defects. A milestone not answered within that period is deemed approved.

**4. Revisions.** Two rounds of revisions per milestone are included in the fee. Each further round is charged at 70 per hour. Any change to the scope in clause 1 requires a signed change order stating the added cost and time before work starts.

**5. Fees paid by third parties.** The Client shall hold, and pay for, the Apple Developer Program membership (99 per year) and the Google Play developer account (one-time 25). The Client shall also pay the push notification provider's charges (current plan: free up to 10,000 messages per month, then at the provider's published rates).

**6. Price.** 11,400, in three payments of 3,800: on signing, on Milestone A and on Milestone B.
