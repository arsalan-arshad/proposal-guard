# Consulting Services Agreement

**Between** Calder & Moss Advisory ("Consultant") **and** Pellam Foods Ltd ("Client").

**1. Services.** The Consultant shall provide operational consulting services in respect of the Client's warehouse process, including such analysis, workshops and recommendations as the Client may reasonably require from time to time.

**2. Term.** This Agreement begins on 1 June and continues until the Services are completed to the Client's satisfaction.

**3. Deliverables.** The Consultant shall deliver (a) a written current-state assessment of no more than 20 pages; (b) a prioritised list of recommendations; and (c) any other materials necessary for implementation.

**4. Acceptance.** Deliverables are accepted when the Client confirms in writing that they meet its requirements. Acceptance shall not be unreasonably withheld.

**5. Fees.** The Client shall pay the Consultant 9,500, invoiced in two equal instalments, the first on signature and the second on delivery of the assessment.

**6. Expenses.** Travel and accommodation shall be reimbursed at cost on presentation of receipts.

**7. Governing law.** This Agreement is governed by the laws of England and Wales.
