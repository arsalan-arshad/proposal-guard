# Translation Agreement — Spanish to English, Training Manual

1. The Translator will translate the Client's operations manual (approx. 22,000 words, supplied as Word files) from Spanish to English.

2. Deadline: 24 working days after receipt of all files.

3. Rate: 0.09 USD per source word, invoiced on delivery. A glossary supplied by the Client will be followed; terms not in the glossary will be marked in comments for the Client's decision.

4. The Client may request corrections to mistranslation or omission within 10 working days of delivery. These are made free of charge. Preference changes, such as stylistic rewrites of accepted text, are charged at 28 USD per hour.

5. Confidentiality: the Translator will not share the manual and will delete working files 60 days after final payment.

6. If the Client does not respond within the 10 working days stated in clause 4, the translation will be considered final.
