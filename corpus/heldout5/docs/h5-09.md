# Podcast Production — "The Long Table"

Host of the show: Delphine Aubert. Producer: Wren & Co Audio.

## Episodes

Wren & Co will edit and mix 10 episodes of about 40 minutes each. Delphine, as host, records interviews on her own schedule and uploads raw files to a shared folder.

## Work included per episode

- Remove long pauses and obvious stumbles
- Level the voices and cut background hum
- Add the intro and outro music supplied by the client
- Write a short description for the feed

## Subscription note

The show's listeners can sign up to a paid members' feed, a subscription that Delphine sells through her own platform. Wren & Co does not manage that feed, billing or customer service.

## Turnaround

Each episode is returned within 5 working days of upload. Delphine listens and replies with notes within 3 days; if no reply comes, the episode is considered approved for release.

## Revisions

One round of notes per episode. If she wants to rework an episode after that, it is 45 USD per hour in 15-minute steps.

## Fees and costs

550 USD per episode, invoiced monthly. The audio hosting plan for the feed is Delphine's. Wren & Co's own editing software is covered by the studio.
