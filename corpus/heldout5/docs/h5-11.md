# CONSULTING AGREEMENT

This agreement is made between Ostrander Advisory ("Consultant") and Pell Street Bakery Group ("Client").

**1. Services**

1.1 Consultant shall provide ongoing support and guidance to improve Client's operations and online presence, as well as such other assistance as Client may reasonably need from time to time.

1.2 Consultant shall use best efforts to deliver a seamless, best-in-class customer experience across all channels.

**2. Fees**

2.1 Client shall pay a monthly retainer of 2,500 USD, due on the first of each month.

2.2 Consultant may invoice for expenses incurred on Client's behalf, including software subscriptions, advertising spend, SMS credits and any tools required.

**3. Acceptance**

3.1 Work product shall be subject to Client's approval. Client shall approve or reject deliverables in good faith.

3.2 Payment of the retainer is not conditional on approval.

**4. Revisions**

4.1 Consultant will make revisions to deliverables until Client is satisfied.

4.2 Additional services beyond the scope above shall be agreed in due course.

**5. Term**

5.1 This agreement continues until terminated by either party on 30 days' notice.
