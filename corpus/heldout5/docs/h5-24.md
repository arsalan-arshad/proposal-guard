# Project Outline: "Trailhead" Hiking Companion App

Prepared by Nolan Bridges for the founders of Trailhead.

## 1. Vision
A friendly hiking companion app for iOS and Android that helps people find trails, record hikes and share them with friends. I'll aim to make it intuitive, delightful and fast.

## 2. Features
- Trail search with filters
- Offline maps
- Hike recording with a simple summary
- Friend sharing
- Photos on the map
- Other nice touches as they come to mind during the build

## 3. Maps and data
Trail data comes from a public dataset. The offline map tiles come from a tile provider; their pricing is by monthly active user and goes up as the app grows. Weather comes from a weather API. We'll decide later how these are set up.

## 4. Backend
I'll set up a server for accounts and shared hikes. A managed database and file storage for photos will be needed, plus push notifications for friend requests.

## 5. Accounts
App store listings need developer accounts for both stores, with yearly fees.

## 6. Timeline
Approximately 4 months. Milestone demos each month; you tell me how they look and I adjust.

## 7. Fees
Fixed fee of 22,000 USD, paid monthly in four parts. If the app ends up needing more work than this we will find a fair arrangement.

## 8. Support
After launch I'll fix issues as they appear and help with updates. This is part of the fee and I will be available.

## 9. Ownership
Code is yours when the last payment arrives.
