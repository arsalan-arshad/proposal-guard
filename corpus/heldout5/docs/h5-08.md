# Proposal: Customer Portal for SmoothRoute Logistics

Prepared for the operations team at SmoothRoute Logistics, based in Quickwater Bay.

## Background

SmoothRoute currently sells an "Unlimited Pickups" plan to its small-business customers and wants a portal where those customers can see their pickup history. This proposal covers only the portal; the plan itself is not changed.

## What I will build

- Login page with email and password
- Pickup history list, filterable by date range and by depot
- Downloadable CSV of the history, columns fixed as listed in Appendix A
- Admin screen where SmoothRoute staff can disable a customer account

## Out of scope

The billing for the Unlimited Pickups plan, mobile apps, and any integration with the Fast Cloud Dispatch system beyond reading its nightly export file.

## Schedule and fees

Build: 6 weeks from kickoff. Fee: 7,400 USD in three equal parts, on kickoff, on delivery of the staging site, and on go-live.

Hosting for the portal is expected to run about 45 USD a month on SmoothRoute's existing cloud account. SmoothRoute pays this directly.

## Review

SmoothRoute has 6 business days after each delivery to report problems in writing. After that the delivery is treated as accepted.

## Changes

Up to 2 rounds of copy and layout changes on the finished screens are included. Further changes are billed at 80 USD per hour and start once SmoothRoute approves the estimate.
