# Workshop Proposal: Spreadsheet Skills for Finance Teams

Prepared for Aldermoor Credit Union, learning and development team.

## Format

- 2 half-day sessions (3.5 hours each), on-site or video call, up to 14 participants
- Participants bring their own laptops
- Hands-on exercises from a dummy loan book I will create

## Topics

- Lookups and joins with XLOOKUP
- Pivot tables for monthly reporting
- Data checks: duplicates, blanks, outliers
- A short section on building a one-page dashboard

## Materials

Each participant gets a PDF workbook of 30 pages and the exercise files. I may add bonus material if the group is keen.

## Costs

- Fee: 3,200 GBP for both sessions
- Travel, if on-site: Aldermoor covers train fare and one night's hotel
- Video platform: Aldermoor provides the meeting link and licence
- Any paid add-in a participant wants to install is their own choice and cost

## After the sessions

I'll be around for questions by email for a while afterwards, whatever people need.

## Feedback on the plan

Please tell me about changes to the topic list at least 10 days before the first session. After that I'll run the plan as written.
