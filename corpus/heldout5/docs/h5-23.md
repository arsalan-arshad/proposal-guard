# Master Services Agreement — Clause Library Tool for Marlowe & Pike Solicitors

**1. Definitions**
"Tool" means the web-based clause library described in Schedule 1. "Go-Live" means the date the Tool is first available to Marlowe & Pike staff in the production environment.

**2. Services**
2.1 The Developer will build a searchable library of the Firm's own precedent clauses, with tagging, version history for each clause, and export to Word.
2.2 The library will support up to 25 named users and up to 5,000 clauses.
2.3 Search results will return within 2 seconds for 95% of queries on a library of 5,000 clauses, measured on the staging environment.

**3. Payment Schedule**
3.1 20% on signature of this agreement.
3.2 30% on delivery of the staging build.
3.3 40% on Acceptance.
3.4 10% 30 days after Go-Live.

**4. Acceptance**
4.1 The Firm will test the staging build against Schedule 2 (the acceptance checklist) within 10 business days of delivery.
4.2 The Firm will either accept in writing or list each failed item. The Developer will correct failed items within 10 business days and the Firm will have 5 business days to retest.
4.3 If the Firm does not respond within the periods in 4.1 or 4.2, the build is deemed accepted.

**5. Third-party services**
5.1 The Firm will hold, and pay for, the cloud hosting account and the single sign-on service.
5.2 The Developer will use an open-source search engine and no licence fees arise.
5.3 If the Firm wishes to add an AI drafting feature through a model provider's API, that is outside this agreement, and the Firm would pay the provider's usage fees directly.

**6. Change control**
6.1 Two rounds of interface changes after the staging build are included.
6.2 All other changes require a written change order showing price and effect on dates, at the rate of 110 GBP per hour, and are not started until the Firm signs it.
