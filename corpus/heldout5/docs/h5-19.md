# Email and SMS Automation — Proposal for Juniper Dental

## Overview
I will set up appointment reminders and a recall campaign for Juniper Dental's patients, using the practice software's export and a messaging platform.

## Workflows
1. Appointment reminder: email 48 hours before, SMS 3 hours before
2. Six-month recall: email then SMS 7 days later if no booking
3. Post-visit survey: one email 1 day after the visit, one question

## Sending costs
SMS messages are priced by the messaging provider at around 0.008 USD per message. The email platform tier needed for 3,000 contacts is about 35 USD per month. Both accounts will be opened in the practice's name and billed to Juniper's card.

## Test period
For 14 days after launch I'll watch delivery logs and fix wording or timing problems. At the end of the 14 days, the practice manager confirms in writing, or the setup is considered handed over.

## Edits
Message wording: 3 rounds across all workflows. Additional workflows or rewrites after that are 75 USD per hour, with a quote first.

## Price
2,100 USD, 50% to start, 50% at handover.
