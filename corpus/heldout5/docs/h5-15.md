# Design Services Agreement — Garage Conversion Drawings

Parties: Alder & Reed Drafting ("Drafter") and Mr. and Mrs. Lindqvist ("Owners").

**1. Scope of drawings**

1.1 The Drafter will prepare measured drawings of the existing garage and a proposed plan for its conversion to a studio, consisting of: floor plan, two elevations, one section, and a window and door schedule, all at 1:50.

1.2 Drawings will be delivered as PDF and DWG files.

1.3 Structural calculations, planning applications and interior design are not included.

**2. Site visit**

2.1 One site visit of up to 3 hours is included. A second visit, if needed because of access problems, is charged at 55 EUR plus travel at cost.

**3. Review**

3.1 The Owners will review each draft within 7 calendar days of receipt and give comments in one message.

3.2 If the Owners do not respond within that time, the Drafter will treat the draft as approved and continue.

**4. Revisions**

4.1 Two revision rounds are included in the fee. A round means one set of consolidated comments.

4.2 Each further round is charged at 48 EUR per hour with an estimate given first.

**5. Costs**

5.1 The Owners pay the CAD plotting and print charges at the print shop and any council fee.

5.2 The Drafter bears the cost of CAD software and licences.

**6. Fee**

6.1 1,450 EUR: 40% on signing, 60% on approval of the final drawings.
