# Services Agreement — Brightwater Pottery Online Shop

Between Halloran Digital Works ("Contractor") and Brightwater Pottery Ltd ("Client").

## 1. Deliverables

1.1 Contractor will build a storefront with: a product catalogue of up to 60 items, a cart and checkout connected to the Client's existing payment account, and four static pages (About, Shipping, Returns, Contact).

1.2 Each page will score at least 85 on the Lighthouse performance audit on a mid-range phone profile at hand-over.

1.3 Anything not listed in 1.1 is outside this agreement.

## 2. Review and acceptance

2.1 Contractor will deliver each milestone by email. Client has 5 business days from delivery to accept it or send a written list of defects against clause 1.

2.2 If Client sends no response within those 5 business days, the milestone is deemed accepted and the related invoice falls due.

2.3 Defects against clause 1 are fixed at no charge. Requests that are not defects follow clause 4.

## 3. Third-party costs

3.1 Client pays directly: domain renewal (about 15 USD per year), the e-commerce platform plan (Client's account, billed monthly), and the transactional email service.

3.2 Contractor pays for the stock photo licence used on the home page and will not pass it on.

## 4. Changes

4.1 The price includes 2 rounds of design revisions per page, each round consolidated in a single document.

4.2 A third round, or any additional page, is quoted in writing at 65 USD per hour before work begins. No work starts until Client confirms the quote by email.

## 5. Payment

5.1 30% on signature, 40% on acceptance of milestone one, 30% on acceptance of milestone two. Invoices are payable within 14 days.
