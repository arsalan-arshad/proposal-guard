# Statement of Work — Invoice Reconciliation Module

Client: Pemberton Hardware Wholesale
Supplier: Calloway Software Studio
Version 2

## 1. Background
Pemberton uses an off-the-shelf accounting package and reconciles supplier invoices by hand. This SOW adds an import-and-match module on top of it.

## 2. Deliverables
2.1 CSV/PDF invoice import for the 6 suppliers listed in Appendix B.
2.2 Automatic matching of invoice lines to purchase orders, with a "needs review" queue for anything under 95% field match.
2.3 A monthly exceptions report in the format of the sample sheet.
2.4 Plain admin guide, up to 10 pages.

## 3. Not included
Additional supplier formats, payroll, tax filing, anything on the sales ledger.

## 4. Environment
4.1 The module runs inside Pemberton's own server room. No new hosting is needed.
4.2 The OCR service used to read scanned PDFs is billed per page by the vendor; Pemberton opens the account and pays the bill. Estimated 0.015 USD per page.
4.3 The accounting package's API add-on, required for 2.2, costs 29 USD per month and is Pemberton's.

## 5. Acceptance
5.1 Calloway delivers to a test copy of the ledger. Pemberton's finance lead has 10 working days to run the 120 sample invoices and either accept or list failures.
5.2 Failures in 2.1 to 2.3 are corrected by Calloway at no cost, and the 10-day window restarts for the corrected items only.
5.3 If no reply arrives by day 10, the delivery is accepted.

## 6. Changes
6.1 Calloway includes 3 small adjustments to the report layout.
6.2 Anything else needs a change order stating hours and price, signed by both sides, before work begins. Rate is 85 USD per hour.

## 7. Price and schedule
18,500 USD fixed. 25% at signing, 25% at start of testing, 50% on acceptance. Delivery to test within 9 weeks of signing.
