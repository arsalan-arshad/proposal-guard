# Website Maintenance Retainer

Provider: Anselm Reid Web Services
Customer: Quillon Books

## What the retainer covers

- Monthly plugin and core updates on the customer's WordPress site
- Daily off-site backups, retained for 30 days
- Uptime check every 5 minutes, with an e-mail alert to the customer
- Up to 2 hours of small content edits each month, unused hours do not roll over

## Payment

The monthly fee is 180 USD, billed on the 1st. Invoices are due within 14 days.

## What is not covered

Redesigns, new features, migrations and anything over the 2 hours are quoted separately before work begins.

## Term

Rolling monthly. Either side can end it with 30 days' written notice.

## Liability

The provider's liability is limited to the fees paid in the preceding three months.
