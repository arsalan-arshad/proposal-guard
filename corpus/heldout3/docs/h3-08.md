# Terms of Engagement: Landing Page for Smoothie Station

Smoothie Station is a juice bar chain. This page covers the landing page we're building for its new "Unlimited Sips" plan, a monthly drink pass the chain sells to its own customers.

## The work

1. One responsive landing page, with hero section, pricing block for the Unlimited Sips plan, FAQ of 6 questions, and a signup form.
2. Signup form posts to the chain's existing subscription system. We do not touch billing.
3. Copy supplied by Smoothie Station; we proof it once for typos.

## Timing

- Wireframe: 3 working days after kickoff
- Build: 7 working days after wireframe is confirmed
- Wireframe confirmation is due from Smoothie Station within 2 working days. After that the schedule moves day for day.

## Change handling

Two rounds of design changes are included. After that any change is billed at 70 USD an hour, and we send an estimate first.

## Costs not in our fee

Hosting on the chain's CloudNine account, the domain and the SSL certificate are paid by Smoothie Station.

Fee: 2,150 USD.
