# Mobile App Development Agreement

This Agreement is made between Tidewater Fitness Inc. ("Client") and Kwame Adjei ("Developer").

## 1. Deliverables

1.1 The Developer shall deliver an iOS and Android app with the following screens: login, workout list, workout detail, progress chart, settings.
1.2 The app shall support 2 languages: English and French.
1.3 Source code shall be delivered in a private repository owned by the Client.

## 2. Milestones and acceptance

2.1 Milestone 1 (login, workout list) is delivered by week 4. Milestone 2 (all remaining screens) by week 9.
2.2 The Client shall accept or reject each milestone in writing within 7 days of delivery, stating the reasons for any rejection by reference to Section 1.
2.3 Failing a written rejection within 7 days, the milestone is deemed accepted and the corresponding invoice is payable.

## 3. Accounts and fees of third parties

3.1 The Client shall hold the Apple Developer Program and Google Play Console accounts and bear their annual fees.
3.2 The Client shall bear the costs of any push-notification, analytics or map service used by the app.

## 4. Change requests

4.1 Each milestone includes two rounds of corrections to the agreed screens.
4.2 Any other change shall be set out in a written change order stating the additional hours, fee and effect on the schedule, and no work on it begins until the Client signs it.

## 5. Fees

Fixed fee 14,500 USD in three instalments: 30%, 35%, 35%.
