# SOW — Marketing Automation Setup for Kestrel Outdoor Gear

## Background

Kestrel Outdoor Gear sells camping equipment online and wants its lead follow-up automated. Kestrel also runs a "Smooth Ride" gear rental subscription for its own customers; that product is outside this project.

## Work items

1. Connect the website forms to HubSpot (3 forms).
2. Build a lead scoring model with 6 rules agreed on in the kickoff meeting.
3. Create 4 e-mail sequences, 3 messages each.
4. Build a weekly report dashboard with 8 named metrics.
5. Train 2 staff members in a single 90-minute session.

## Schedule

Weeks 1 and 2: items 1 and 2. Weeks 3 and 4: items 3 and 4. Week 5: item 5.

## Approval

Kestrel's marketing lead approves each work item in writing. Approvals are due 5 working days after the item is delivered; if Kestrel needs longer it will tell us before the 5 days run out, and the schedule extends by the same number of days.

## Revisions and extra work

Every work item includes 1 round of changes. Additional rounds, further sequences or extra dashboard metrics are quoted separately at 85 USD per hour with a capped estimate.

## Software and services

Kestrel holds the HubSpot subscription and the e-mail sending domain, and pays for them. Our fee covers labour only.

## Fee

6,000 USD, split evenly across weeks 1, 3 and 5.
