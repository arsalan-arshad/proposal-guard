3. A one-page follow-up summary sent within 3 working days.

## 2. Venue and materials

5. Any paid video-conferencing seats needed for remote attendees are arranged by the Client.

## 3. Satisfaction

6. The Client will let the Facilitator know if the workshop was acceptable.
7. The Facilitator will make adjustments to the content until the Client is satisfied.

## 4. Fee

8. 3,200 CHF, payable on the day.
9. Rescheduling with less than 10 days' notice incurs a 500 CHF fee.
