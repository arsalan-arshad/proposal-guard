# Consulting Agreement — Data Pipeline Review

**Between:** Ostrander Logistics GmbH ("Client") and Marguerite Eze Consulting ("Consultant")

## Article 1. Services

1.1 The Consultant shall provide ongoing support for the Client's analytics platform and shall make improvements as needed to ensure optimal performance.
1.2 The Consultant shall assist with other data-related tasks as may be requested from time to time.
1.3 The Consultant will deliver a written findings report and a prioritised remediation list.

## Article 2. Acceptance

2.1 The Client shall approve the findings report before the second instalment becomes payable.
2.2 Final sign-off by the Client's data lead is required before the engagement closes.

## Article 3. Fees

3.1 The fee is 9,000 EUR, invoiced in two instalments.
3.2 Cloud warehouse usage charges incurred during the review shall be borne as the parties agree.

## Article 4. Changes

4.1 The Client may request changes to the report and the Consultant shall implement them promptly and to the Client's satisfaction.

## Article 5. Governing law

This agreement is governed by the laws of Germany.
