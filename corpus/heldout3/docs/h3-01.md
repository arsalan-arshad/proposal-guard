# Statement of Work: Brightwell Pottery Online Shop

Prepared by Nadia Kowalczyk for Brightwell Pottery Ltd.

## 1. Deliverables

1.1 A Shopify store using the Dawn theme, customised with the supplied logo, two brand colours and one font.
1.2 Up to 40 product listings entered from the client's spreadsheet, each with up to 5 images.
1.3 Three static pages: About, Shipping, Contact.
1.4 Checkout configured for card payments and one local bank transfer option.

## 2. Timeline

Design draft within 8 working days of receiving the logo and spreadsheet. Launch within 20 working days of the start date.

## 3. Review and acceptance

3.1 The Client will review each deliverable within 5 working days of delivery and send one consolidated list of corrections.
3.2 If no reply arrives within 5 working days, the deliverable is treated as accepted and the milestone invoice falls due.

## 4. Revisions

4.1 Two rounds of revisions per deliverable are included.
4.2 Further rounds, or requests outside section 1, are quoted at 45 USD per hour after a written estimate that the Client approves before work starts.

## 5. Third-party costs

5.1 The Client pays the Shopify subscription, the domain registration and any paid theme or app licences directly to the vendors.
5.2 The Contractor will not purchase anything on the Client's behalf without written approval.

## 6. Payment

40% on signing, 30% at design approval, 30% at launch. Invoices are payable within 10 days.
