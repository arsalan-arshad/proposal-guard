# Maintenance Agreement

The monthly retainer covers up to 10 hours of development work.
Requests are answered within 1 business day.
The client pays for hosting on Railway and for the domain name.
Work beyond the retainer is quoted as a change request.
The client approves each monthly report within 5 days; otherwise it is deemed approved.
Two rounds of revisions are included per report.
The service will be available 99.9% of the time, measured monthly.
