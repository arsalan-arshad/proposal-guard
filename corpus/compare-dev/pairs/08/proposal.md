Proposal: warehouse process review

Scope
I will carry out a two-day site visit, interview shift supervisors, analyse your layout and slotting data and deliver a written report with prioritised recommendations and a roadmap.

Method
Day one is receiving, day two is picking. I will ask for your last three months of order data in a spreadsheet before the visit so that I can arrive with specific questions.

Deliverables
- A report of about 20 pages
- A one-hour readout with your operations team
- An annotated layout drawing showing proposed changes

Schedule
Draft report after 4 weeks; final report after 5 weeks from the start date.

Review
Your operations director reviews the draft within 5 business days and sends comments. I include one round of revisions to the draft. Requests that go beyond the scope above are quoted separately.

Fees
A fixed fee, invoiced half at the start and half on delivery of the final report, each due within 30 days.
