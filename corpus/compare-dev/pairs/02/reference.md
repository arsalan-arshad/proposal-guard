REQUEST FOR PROPOSALS: Field Inspection Mobile Application
Issued by: Harbourline Utilities Cooperative (fictional)

1. Background
Harbourline Utilities Cooperative seeks a vendor to design and build a mobile application for field inspectors who record pipe and valve conditions on site.

2. Scope
The application shall support offline data capture, photo attachment, GPS tagging and synchronisation with the cooperative's inventory database. The vendor shall also provide training materials and any other documentation reasonably required for adoption.

3. Infrastructure
The back-end shall run in the cooperative's existing AWS account. The cooperative will pay all AWS charges. The vendor shall use the cooperative's Apple and Google developer accounts for publication.

4. Schedule
The completed application shall be delivered within 12 weeks of contract signature.

5. Acceptance
Each release candidate is subject to written acceptance by the cooperative's operations manager. Payment of each milestone invoice follows acceptance.

6. Payment terms
Undisputed invoices are payable within 30 days of receipt.

7. Submission
Proposals must describe the team, methodology, timeline and fees. Questions may be sent to the procurement office until the closing date.
