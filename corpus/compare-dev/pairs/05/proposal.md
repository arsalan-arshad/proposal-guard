Proposal: address cleaning and delivery zone analysis

Approach
I will profile the CSV first and send you a short list of the problem patterns. Then I clean and standardise addresses with documented rules, so every change can be traced.

Geocoding
Cleaned addresses are geocoded and plotted on an interactive map. I will confirm the geocoding provider with you before running anything.

Delivery notes
The free-text delivery notes are categorised using a language model API. Usage for the OpenAI API is billed to your own account, so you control the spend. I will estimate the volume beforehand, and only a de-identified sample of notes is sent.

Outputs
- Cleaned CSV and a change log
- Delivery zone sheets
- A report of about six pages

Timeline
