# Podcast website refresh: "Late Shift Stories"

Our podcast is hosted by two night nurses who interview people working unusual hours. Episodes come out every Thursday. We want a cleaner website where listeners can find episodes quickly.

## What we have
- The episode archive on a free podcast host (we do not need a new host or any paid hosting)
- Artwork and a logo from a volunteer designer
- Our code is under an open-source licence, since we published the old theme on our public repository under the MIT licence

## What we want
- A homepage with the latest episode and a search box
- Pages for each episode with show notes, transcript and a player
- A page about the hosts
- A newsletter signup box that connects to the tool we already use

Please do not make us buy extra plug-ins. We have no budget for third-party subscriptions at the moment.

We are domain experts in nursing, not in web work, so explain things plainly.

We would like to see a draft in 3 weeks.
