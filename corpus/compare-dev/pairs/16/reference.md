Request for Quotation: Loyalty app for a coffee chain

Brewhouse Collective operates fourteen cafes. We want a loyalty app for iOS and Android with stamp cards, offers and push notifications.

Requirements
1. Customer sign-up with phone number verified by an SMS code
2. Digital stamp card and reward redemption at the till
3. Push notifications for offers
4. A simple back-office web page for managers
5. Publication to the App Store and Google Play under the app name "Brewhouse"
6. Analytics for marketing, plus integrations with other tools where useful

Process
Our head of marketing accepts each milestone. Milestone payments become due once she has accepted the build.

Terms
- Delivery: completion within 16 weeks of the start date.
- Acceptance: three working days to test and accept each build.
- Payment: net 30.
- Change management: out-of-scope requests are quoted separately.

Bids should state the total fixed price, the team and relevant references.
