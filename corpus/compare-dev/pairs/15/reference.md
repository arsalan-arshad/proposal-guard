Newsletter writer and setup for a community garden

We are Hollow Lane Community Garden, a volunteer-run charity. We would like a monthly newsletter that goes to about 900 supporters.

What we need
- A simple newsletter template that matches our logo and colours
- The first three issues written by you, from notes and photos we supply
- Set-up of the sending account so our volunteers can send future issues

Sending: we already have a Mailgun account for email delivery and the charity pays the bill. Please connect the newsletter to it, and we will send you the credentials safely when we start.

Please include the following in your plan: 2 rounds of revisions per issue, and a day-by-day plan for the first issue.

Timing: we will review each draft within 3 business days. We expect the first issue to be ready 10 days after you start, and the next issues every four weeks.

Payment: invoices are due 14 days after issue.

We cannot pay a large fee, so a clear fixed price is best.
