# Campaign brief: Spring Loyalty Push

**Client:** Fernwood Garden Centres (fictional)
**Channel mix:** email, SMS, social

| Item | Detail |
|---|---|
| Audience | Existing loyalty members, about 12,000 |
| Email | Three-email sequence promoting the spring plant sale |
| SMS | One reminder text on the final weekend of the sale, sent through Twilio |
| Social | Six posts for Instagram and Facebook |
| Reporting | One summary report after the campaign |
| Approval | Marketing director approves each asset before it is sent |

## Notes
We want the tone to be friendly and knowledgeable, never pushy. Creative should be tailored to each channel and refreshed as needed during the campaign.

Please quote the fee for creative and planning. The campaign starts on 14 April and the sale runs for ten days.

Our brand guide covers colours, fonts and tone of voice. Photography comes from our own library.

Payment terms are net 30 from invoice.
