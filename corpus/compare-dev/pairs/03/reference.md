CONTENT SERVICES AGREEMENT (excerpt)

1. Services. The Contractor shall write ten (10) long-form articles of approximately 1,500 words each on topics supplied by the Company, for publication on the Company blog.

2. Revisions. The Company may request two rounds of revisions per article. Each round is limited to changes of tone, structure or factual correction. Additional rounds requested by the Company shall be charged at the Contractor's hourly rate.

3. Delivery. The Contractor shall deliver each article within 10 days of receiving the topic brief.

4. Approval. The Company shall review each article and notify the Contractor of its approval or comments within 5 business days of receipt. Silence for 5 business days shall be treated as acceptance.

5. Fees. The fee is a fixed amount per article. The Contractor shall invoice on delivery, and the Company shall pay within 21 days of the invoice date.

6. Ownership. Rights in the finished articles transfer to the Company on full payment.

7. Confidentiality. Each party shall keep the other's non-public information confidential.
