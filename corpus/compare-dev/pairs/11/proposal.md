Response to Schedule A: Customer Portal

We have reviewed the draft Schedule A and propose the following terms for the Customer Portal.

1. Scope. We will build the Portal as listed in the Statement of Work, version 3. Integrations are limited to those named in the Statement of Work.

2. Delivery. We will complete the Portal within 14 weeks of the effective date. We will share a staging link every two weeks.

3. Review. The Customer reviews each staged release within 10 days. If the Customer does not respond within 10 days, the release is treated as approved.

4. Payment. Each invoice is due net 30.

5. Revisions. Two rounds of revisions per release are included in the Fees. Further revisions are handled as change orders and billed at the rate in the Rate Card.

6. Hosting. Hosting on a cloud platform will be on the Customer's account and the Customer will pay the charges. We will recommend a platform in the first two weeks.

7. Documentation. We will provide a technical runbook and admin guide at handover.

We are happy to discuss any of these points.
