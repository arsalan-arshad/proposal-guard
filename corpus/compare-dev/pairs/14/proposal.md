Wedding film proposal

Thank you for considering me; a barn wedding in June sounds beautiful.

On the day
Six hours of coverage with two cameras and a lapel microphone for the ceremony and speeches.

Films
- A 4 to 5 minute highlight film with licensed music
- The full ceremony as one continuous file

Editing and notes
I send a first cut for your notes. Your notes can ask for cuts, reordering and music changes. The price includes 1 revision round after the first cut. Further rounds are charged at an hourly rate.

Timing
I complete and deliver the final film within 8 weeks of the wedding. You get the first cut after about 5 weeks. I ask you to send your notes within 5 days.

Payment
30% deposit when you book. The balance is due within 7 days of the final film.

Storage
I deliver through the shared folder you set up.
