# Marketing Automation Setup

Prepared for Olive & Ash Interiors

## Overview

We will set up a complete, best-in-class e-mail marketing system that grows with the business and engages customers across the full lifecycle. The setup includes welcome flows, abandoned cart reminders, win-back messages and whatever else is useful for the shop.

## Work plan

- Audit of the current list and sign-up forms
- Build the automation flows and templates
- Ongoing optimisation to keep results strong
- Training for the team and general support

## Costs

Monthly management: 650 USD. The e-mail platform plan, SMS credits and the integration app subscription are needed to run everything and are set up by us.

## Sign-off

Each flow goes live once approved by the client.

## Changes

Flows and templates are adjusted as many times as the client wants during the engagement.
