# Terms of Work — Online Store Build

**Parties:** Rafferty & Sons Garden Supply ("Client"); Kwame Asante, freelance developer ("Developer").

**1. The work**
1.1 Developer builds a product catalogue of up to 150 SKUs with filters for category, price and pot size.
1.2 Developer will make the site look attractive and modern, with a smooth experience on all devices.
1.3 Developer will integrate the payment provider and a shipping rate calculator.

**2. Payment milestones**
2.1 25% on signing.
2.2 25% on Client approval of the design.
2.3 50% on approval of the finished store.
2.4 Approval means written confirmation from the Client.

**3. Third parties**
3.1 Shopify plan, payment processing fees, the shipping rate app and the domain name are for the Client's account.

**4. Changes**
4.1 Developer will carry out up to 3 rounds of changes to the design.
4.2 After the third round, changes are charged at 60 USD per hour.
