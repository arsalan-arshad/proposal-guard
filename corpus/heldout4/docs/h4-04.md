# CONSULTING SERVICES AGREEMENT

## Article 1. Services

1.1 The Consultant shall provide ongoing support for the Client's data platform, including improvements as needed, performance tuning, and general advice on best practices.

1.2 The Consultant shall make reasonable efforts to deliver a robust, scalable and user-friendly solution.

1.3 Additional services may be requested by the Client from time to time and shall be performed by the Consultant.

## Article 2. Approval

2.1 Deliverables are subject to approval by the Client's project lead.

2.2 Payment of each instalment is conditional upon such approval.

## Article 3. Charges

3.1 The monthly retainer is 3,000 EUR.

3.2 Cloud warehouse compute, third-party connectors and any licences required for the Services shall be procured as necessary for the proper performance of this Agreement.

## Article 4. Amendments

4.1 The Consultant shall implement amendments requested by the Client until the Client is satisfied.

4.2 This Agreement is governed by the laws agreed between the parties.
