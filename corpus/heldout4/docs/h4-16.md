# Logo and Brand Kit — Proposal

Client: Stonecress Veterinary Clinic
Designer: Annelise Duarte

## Package

| Item | Quantity | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| Logo concepts | 3 | presented as one PDF |
| Chosen concept refined | 1 | 2 refinement rounds |
| Colour palette | 1 | 5 colours with hex and CMYK values |
| Typeface pairing | 1 | fonts chosen are free or already licensed by the client |
| Business card layout | 1 | print-ready PDF |

## Schedule

Concepts: 5 working days after the briefing call.
Client picks a concept within 5 working days of receiving the PDF. If no choice has been made by then, the work pauses and the schedule shifts day for day.

## Fee

1,500 USD. Half at the start, half on delivery of final files. Printing is not included and is paid by the clinic to the printer.

## Extra work

A third refinement round or extra concept is 150 USD.
