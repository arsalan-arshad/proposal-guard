# Project Brief: Smoothie Society Shop Rebuild

Smooth Operator Digital for Smoothie Society (a subscription box company that ships blended-fruit kits)

Note: the "subscription" below is the product the client sells to its customers, not a service I'm billing for.

## What we will build

- Product page template with a size picker (small, medium, family) and a delivery-day picker
- Checkout that supports the client's weekly and fortnightly boxes
- A skip-a-week button in the customer account area
- Order confirmation e-mail template

## Timeline

Kick-off 3 March. Staging link by 24 March. Launch 7 April.

## Money

Fixed fee 4,800 USD: 40% on signing, 30% at staging link, 30% on launch.
Theme licence (49 USD one-off) is paid by Smoothie Society directly to the theme vendor.
The payment gateway and transactional e-mail service fees are paid by Smoothie Society.

## Review

The client reviews the staging link within 4 working days of receiving it. If we hear nothing by then, we go ahead to launch on the date above.

## Changes

Up to 2 rounds of layout changes at staging, each as a single combined list. Anything beyond that, or any new feature, gets a written quote before work starts.
