# Statement of Work: Ledgerly Mobile App

**Between:** Northwind Tailoring Co-op (the Client) and Mira Kowalczyk, independent developer (the Contractor)

## 1. Deliverables

1.1 The Contractor will build an iOS and Android app with these five screens: sign-in, invoice list, invoice detail, payment reminder settings, and profile.

1.2 The app will load the invoice list in under 3 seconds on a mid-range phone on a 4G connection, measured on the two test devices named in Schedule A.

1.3 Anything not listed in 1.1 is outside this SOW.

## 2. Acceptance

2.1 On delivery of each milestone the Client has 5 business days to test and either accept in writing or send a list of defects against section 1.

2.2 If the Client sends nothing within those 5 business days, the milestone is treated as accepted and the milestone invoice falls due.

## 3. Third-party costs

3.1 The Client pays for the Apple Developer Program and Google Play Console accounts, and the accounts are opened in the Client's name.

3.2 The Client pays the SMS provider's usage charges for payment reminders. The Contractor pays for her own tools and test devices.

## 4. Changes

4.1 Two rounds of revisions per milestone are included in the fee. A round is one consolidated list of changes sent within the review period.

4.2 Further rounds and any new feature are quoted at 70 USD per hour, estimated in writing, and begin only after the Client approves the estimate.
