# Workshop Delivery Agreement

Between Brightfield Training Ltd ("Trainer") and Calloway Logistics ("Host Company").

1. **Programme.** Trainer delivers two one-day workshops on shift-handover communication, each running 09:00 to 16:30, for up to 18 participants per day.

2. **Materials.** Trainer supplies a printed workbook per participant and a slide deck in PDF.

3. **Venue and equipment.** Host Company provides the room, projector and refreshments at its own cost.

4. **Fee.** 2,400 GBP in total. 50% is invoiced on booking and 50% on the day after the second workshop. Invoices are payable within 14 days.

5. **Feedback.** Host Company may send comments on the draft slide deck up to 10 days before the first workshop. One round of changes is included; Trainer will confirm the final deck 5 days before.

6. **Rescheduling.** A date can be moved once at no charge with 14 days' notice. Later changes cost 300 GBP each.

7. **Travel.** Trainer's rail fares are billed at cost with receipts.
