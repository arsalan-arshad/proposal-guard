# DevOps Engagement: Pipeline Modernisation for Quillstone Analytics

## Terms of engagement

**1. Definitions**
1.1 "Pipeline" means the existing build and deploy scripts listed in Appendix 1.
1.2 "Go-live" means the first production deployment through the new pipeline.

**2. Scope of work**
2.1 Contractor will migrate the 7 services in Appendix 1 to containerised builds with a single deploy command per service.
2.2 Contractor will write a runbook of no more than 10 pages covering rollback, secrets rotation and on-call contacts.
2.3 Anything not named in 2.1 or 2.2 requires a change order under clause 6.

**3. Review**
3.1 Client will review each migrated service within 3 working days of the Contractor's hand-off message.
3.2 A service not rejected in writing within that time is deemed accepted, and the related instalment is payable.

**4. Costs borne by the Client**
4.1 Container registry, CI runner minutes, and cloud compute consumed by the new pipeline.
4.2 Any paid monitoring subscription the Client chooses to add.

**5. Fees**
5.1 Fixed fee 11,000 USD, invoiced in four equal parts on hand-off of services 2, 4, 6 and 7.

**6. Change orders**
6.1 Either party may propose a change in writing. No change takes effect until both sign a change order stating the effect on price and date.
6.2 Unless otherwise agreed, extra work is charged at 120 USD per hour.
