# Fastlane Freight Dashboard Proposal

Prepared for Fastlane Freight Brokers by Ines Barros

Fastlane Freight Brokers will receive a web dashboard showing open loads, carrier status and weekly margin. Their dispatchers use the Unlimited plan from their telematics vendor, which the dashboard reads from, so no change to that plan is needed.

| Item | Detail | Who pays |
|---|---|---|
| Hosting (EU region) | managed app platform, monthly | Fastlane Freight |
| Map tiles API | route display | Fastlane Freight |
| Domain and TLS certificate | dashboard.example-fastlane.test | Ines (year one) |
| Charting library | open source, no fee | n/a |

Timeline: design in week 1, build in weeks 2 to 4, handover in week 5.

Fee: 6,200 USD in three payments. The final payment is due 7 days after handover, whether or not feedback has arrived.

Revisions: the fee includes one round of design changes after week 1 and one round of fixes after handover. Additional work is billed at 85 USD per hour once we both agree the scope in an e-mail.
