# Website Maintenance Plan — Cloudberry Bakery

This plan is for the Cloudberry Bakery website and covers the following, each month:

- Software updates for the site platform and plugins
- Backups kept for 30 days
- Up to 3 content edits (text or image swaps), each completed within 2 business days
- Uptime check every 5 minutes with an e-mail alert to the owner

**Price:** 90 USD per month, billed on the 1st. Cancel with 30 days' notice.

Hosting is billed by the hosting company directly to Cloudberry Bakery. The domain renewal, about 15 USD a year, is also paid by Cloudberry Bakery. A premium plugin licence, if one needs renewing, will be quoted before purchase and paid by the bakery.

Edits beyond 3 in a month are 25 USD each. Anything bigger, like a new page, I will price first and begin only after you reply yes.
