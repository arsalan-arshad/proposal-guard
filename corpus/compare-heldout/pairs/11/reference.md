WEBSITE DEVELOPMENT AGREEMENT (EXCERPT)
Client: Fenwick & Marsh Architects. Developer: the freelancer named in the proposal.

1. Project. The Developer shall build a portfolio website presenting the Client's completed projects, with a filterable gallery, a team page and a contact form.

2. Content. The Client supplies all text and images. The Developer will optimise images for the web.

3. Timeline. The website shall be completed within 6 weeks of receipt of all content.

4. Review. The Client shall review each stage of the work and give written comments within 4 business days of receiving the stage.

5. Payment. The Developer will invoice at the end of each month. Invoices are due net 30.

6. Changes. Changes to the agreed page list require a written change order signed by both parties.

7. Hosting. The Client's website will be hosted on the Client's existing hosting plan, and the Developer will upload the finished site to it.

8. Ownership. Upon payment in full, the Client owns the design and code written for the project, apart from open-source components, which remain under their own licences.

9. Governing law. The laws of the Client's home jurisdiction apply.
