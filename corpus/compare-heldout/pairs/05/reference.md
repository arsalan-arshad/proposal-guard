MASTER SERVICES AGREEMENT: SCHEDULE A (EXTRACT)
Data Cleaning and Reporting Engagement
Between Halden Logistics Ltd ("Client") and the Consultant

1. Services. The Consultant shall clean the Client's shipment dataset (approximately 2 million rows), remove duplicates, standardise the carrier fields and produce a monthly summary report.

2. Deliverables. The cleaned dataset, a data dictionary, and the monthly report template.

3. Acceptance. The Client shall have ten (10) days from delivery to accept or reject each deliverable in writing. A deliverable not rejected in that period is deemed accepted.

4. Revisions. The Consultant shall correct defects reported during the acceptance period. Corrections requested by the Client after acceptance are treated as new work.

5. Fees and payment. The fixed fee is payable in two instalments. Invoices are payable net 45 from the invoice date.

6. Tools. The Consultant may use open-source libraries released under the MIT or Apache licence, provided their licence terms are respected. The Client will supply read access to its data warehouse.

7. Term. The Services will be completed within 8 weeks of the effective date.

8. Confidentiality. All data remains the property of the Client and shall be deleted from the Consultant's systems on completion.
