Larkspur Cycles: film proposal

I am Joaquín Mbeki, an independent director and editor. I have read your brief, and I think the no-voice-over approach suits the craft really well.

Deliverables
- 90 second master film, 16:9, 4K
- 30 second vertical cut
- Colour grade, sound mix and English captions

Plan
Pre-production call, then one shoot day at your workshop. I bring a camera operator and a sound recordist. Rough cut arrives 10 days after the shoot. A fine cut follows, and the finished film is delivered within 4 weeks of the shoot day.

Revisions
Two rounds of revisions are included after the rough cut. After that, small changes are billed at my day rate, quoted before I start, so you always know what you are agreeing to.

Music
I suggest a licensed library track; the licence for web and social use costs a modest one-time fee. I will send three options with the fee for each, and you pick.

Payment
Half on booking the shoot day, half when the final film is delivered. I will send an invoice at each stage.

Thanks, and I look forward to seeing the frames come together.
