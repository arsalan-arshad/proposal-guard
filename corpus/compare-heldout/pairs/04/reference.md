Brand film for Larkspur Cycles: creative brief

Who we are: Larkspur Cycles is a small workshop that builds steel city bikes. We want a 90 second film for our website and social channels.

The idea: follow one frame from raw tube to finished bike, with warm light and real workshop sound. No voice-over, just music and ambient noise.

What we need
- One 90 second master film (16:9)
- A vertical 30 second cut for social
- Colour grade and sound mix
- Captions in English

Shoot: one day in our workshop. We will make the space available and our welder will be on hand.

Revisions: we expect unlimited revisions until the film feels right to the whole team. We are a creative bunch and always find something to tweak.

Timing: we need the finished film delivered within 4 weeks of the shoot day because of a trade show.

Music: we would like a licensed track from a library. Tell us what it would involve.

Payment is by bank transfer after we approve the final film.
