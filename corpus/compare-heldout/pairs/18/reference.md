Job post: Spanish to English translation of a legal-style newsletter

We are a small employment-law practice. Each month we publish a newsletter in Spanish for clients, and we want an English version for our partner firm in another country.

The job
- About 2,500 words each month, starting with the next issue
- Plain, accurate English; no legal advice is added by the translator
- Keep headings and layout

For the first issue we will give you a trial. If you do well, a monthly arrangement follows.

Working agreement
- Delivery within 15 days of receiving each issue
- We include two rounds of revisions for each issue
- Invoices are paid net 30
- You send the translation as an editable document

If our reviewer has questions about particular terms, she will ask in writing and expects you to answer. We keep a list of preferred terms, which you will see in the first message.

Please tell us your per-word rate and your availability around the end of each month, when the newsletter is finalised. We cannot accept machine-translated text with only light editing.

Thank you.
