Ghostwriting proposal: the Brandt memoir

Dear Odette,

It is an honour to be considered. Thirty years of building a tea business is a good story.

Plan
- A kickoff meeting to agree on voice, audience and a chapter outline
- For each of the twelve chapters, one interview of about 90 minutes
- A first draft of each chapter within 2 weeks of its interview
- A full manuscript delivered within 12 weeks of our first meeting, around 60,000 words

I send the first draft of the opening chapter early, so you can check that it sounds like you.

Revisions
The fee covers two revision rounds for the manuscript as a whole, usually one after your first read and one for fact-checking details. If you want a further full rewrite of any chapter, that is quoted as extra work before I start.

Your reading
Because I write ahead of your feedback, a long gap between a chapter and your comments can delay the next one. I would suggest comments within 5 days of each chapter, if that works.

Payment
A deposit, then each chapter is invoiced when delivered, and invoices are payable within 10 days of the invoice date.

Rights
You hold all rights to the text and I am credited only if you wish.

With respect,
Ines Calloway
