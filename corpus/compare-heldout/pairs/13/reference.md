Ghostwriting project: a founder memoir

Who I am: Odette Brandt, founder of a family-run tea importer. I want to write a memoir about building the company over thirty years, aimed at customers and the wider trade.

The book
About 60,000 words, twelve chapters, written in my voice. We will meet to talk, I will send old letters and photographs, and you will shape it into chapters.

How it should work
- We start with a long conversation for each chapter
- You write the chapter and I read it
- I would like three rounds of revisions per chapter, since memory and family details need care
- The complete manuscript is due in 12 weeks from our first meeting

Reading time: I read slowly and want to give it proper attention. I will return comments within 5 days of each chapter.

Money: I will pay a deposit, then pay each chapter on receipt of the invoice, with payment 10 days after the invoice date.

I am not worried about the design of the book itself. A designer will take the final text. A publisher friend suggested an audiobook might follow one day, but that is not part of this project.

If you have written for founders before, please tell me.
