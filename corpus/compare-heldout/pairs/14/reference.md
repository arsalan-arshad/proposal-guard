REQUEST FOR QUOTATION
Marketing Analytics Support, Ashgrove Credit Union

Ashgrove Credit Union (the "Union") invites quotations from independent consultants for marketing analytics support during a twelve month period.

A. Scope of work
A1. Build a monthly dashboard of campaign performance (web, e-mail, branch promotions and so on).
A2. Set up tracking for the new member sign-up form.
A3. Provide recommendations to improve conversion where appropriate.
A4. The dashboard will be built in the Union's own analytics workspace, on its existing software subscription.

B. Commercial terms
B1. The consultant shall invoice monthly. Invoices are payable net 30.
B2. The Union's marketing manager will return feedback on each deliverable within 3 business days.
B3. Revisions: the consultant will include two rounds of revisions to each deliverable.
B4. The tracking setup shall be completed within 3 weeks of the start date.

C. Quotation format
Quotations should be no longer than four pages and should include hourly rates, an outline of the proposed approach and two references. The Union will not accept quotations that rely on tools requiring a new paid licence without a clear explanation of who pays.
