Shopify store migration and loyalty app: request

Company: Verdant Pantry, an online seller of organic dry goods.

We are moving from an old WooCommerce shop to Shopify and want a loyalty points app built at the same time.

Part 1: migration
- Move 300 products, customer accounts and past orders
- Recreate our shipping rules
- Set up redirects so old product links keep working
- Keep the same look, with minor improvements

Part 2: loyalty app
- Customers earn points on every order and redeem them for discounts
- Points shown in the customer account page
- A simple admin view for our team

Practical terms
- Our operations manager must approve the migrated catalogue before we switch the domain; she is part time, so approval timing may vary
- Approval of the loyalty app is also needed before payment
- The whole project should go live within 6 weeks of the start
- We will review work within 2 business days of each delivery
- Invoices are paid net 30
- Two rounds of revisions are included per part

The Shopify plan and the app subscriptions will be paid by us. SMS reminders to customers about points are a possible later addition through a provider such as Twilio.
