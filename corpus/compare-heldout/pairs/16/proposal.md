Greenfurrow Co-op: "Small Farm Stories" production plan

I am a documentary filmmaker who spent years in regional television, and I love small, honest stories. Here is my plan.

Production
Three filming days, one per farm, with a two-person crew: me and a sound recordist. I use natural light and a small kit so it feels unobtrusive for the farmers. I do the interviews myself, slowly, and I always start with a coffee and no camera.

Edit and delivery
- Rough cut of each video about a week after its filming day
- Final videos and social cuts delivered 3 weeks after filming ends
- Subtitles in English for all videos, as burned-in and as separate files

Revisions
One round of revisions on each rough cut, and one on each final cut. More than that, and the extra rounds are billed at my day rate.

Music
I will pick tracks from a stock library on my own subscription, and its cost is part of my fee. You will get the usage licence in writing.

Payment
Half at kickoff and half on final delivery, with payment due net 15.

Reel
My reel link is in the covering email; the second film shows the tone I would use.
