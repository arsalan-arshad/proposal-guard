Operations review: proposal for Rowan Health Partners

Dear Ilse,

Thank you for the request. I am an independent operations consultant with a background in outpatient scheduling.

Method
1. A one-day kickoff to agree the questions and the access I need.
2. Interviews of 30 minutes each with front-desk staff and the five clinic managers.
3. Analysis of the synthetic scheduling summaries you will provide.
4. A written report of up to 20 pages and a 45 minute leadership presentation.

What the report contains
Findings, ranked recommendations with the evidence for each, and a short plan for the first 90 days. Each recommendation names the measure I would use to see whether it worked.

Decisions and review
I will send a draft report two weeks before the presentation. I ask that your committee gives written comments within 5 business days of the draft, and that the sign-off of the final report is given within 5 business days of its delivery. If no reply arrives, I will check in once and then treat the report as accepted.

Scope changes
If you add clinics, new interview groups or a second report, I will write a change order with the price before starting.

Confidentiality
I do not need patient records. I will work only with the summaries and keep them on an encrypted drive.

Regards,
Tobias Lindqvist
