Request: operations review for a regional clinic group

Context
Rowan Health Partners runs five outpatient clinics. Waiting times and rescheduling rates have risen over the past year and we want an outside view of what is going on.

What we would like from a consultant
- A review of how appointments are booked, confirmed and rescheduled
- Interviews with front-desk staff and clinic managers
- A written report with recommendations that follow industry best practices and other appropriate improvements
- A presentation to our leadership team

Process
The final report will be accepted by our steering committee. The committee meets monthly and the report cannot be released to the wider team until the committee has signed off. Payment of the final instalment follows the committee's approval.

Our staff are busy, so interviews should be short. We expect to be flexible about the exact methods the consultant uses and expect them to adapt as needed.

Questions about this request can go to our operations director, Ilse Wanjiru.

Please include references from similar organisations and tell us how you handle patient-related information. We will not share any real patient records for this review; synthetic summaries will be provided.
