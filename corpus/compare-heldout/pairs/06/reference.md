Spring newsletter campaign: what we are after

We are Tidewater Books, an independent bookshop with a loyal local readership and a growing online shop. We want help running a six-week spring campaign built around email.

Goals
- Grow our newsletter list
- Bring more first-time buyers to the web shop
- Promote the author events in April

What the work involves
- Planning a calendar of weekly newsletters
- Writing and designing each one in our existing template
- A sign-up pop-up for the web shop
- A simple report at the end of the campaign

Costs: our Mailchimp plan and the email delivery fees are on our company card, we manage that account and will keep paying for it. Any paid social ads are charged to our own ad account as well.

Please keep the tone like a friendly bookseller, not a corporation. You will have access to the shared photo folder.

Our owner, Gwen, will give feedback on each newsletter. She can be quick or slow depending on events, so build some patience into your plan.

If you have worked with independent shops, say so.
