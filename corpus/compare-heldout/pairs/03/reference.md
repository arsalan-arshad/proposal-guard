REQUEST FOR PROPOSAL
Field Service Mobile Application, Northgate Utilities Cooperative

1. Background
The Cooperative seeks a vendor to design and build a mobile application for its field technicians. Technicians will receive job tickets, log meter readings, capture photos and send customer confirmations.

2. Requirements
2.1 The application shall run on iOS and Android and work offline in areas with poor coverage.
2.2 The application shall integrate with our existing ticketing system, the payroll export, and other related internal systems as required.
2.3 Customers receive appointment reminders and arrival alerts by SMS, which will be delivered through Twilio.
2.4 The backend will be deployed on AWS in the region nearest our depots.
2.5 The solution must be intuitive, robust and easy to use for technicians with limited training.
2.6 Final delivery is subject to acceptance by the Cooperative's operations team.

3. Evaluation
Proposals are scored on relevant experience, technical approach and references. Pricing is considered after the technical review.

4. Submission
Proposals must be received by e-mail by the closing date in the cover letter. Questions are welcome up to one week before closing. Vendors are asked to state team size, availability and any assumptions that affect the price.
