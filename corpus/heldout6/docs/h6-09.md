# Email Automation Setup: Greenfold Subscriptions

Greenfold sells a monthly vegetable box subscription to households. This work sets up the automations that email their customers.

## What we will build

| # | Flow | Trigger | Emails |
|---|------|---------|--------|
| 1 | Welcome | new subscriber | 3 |
| 2 | Skipped box | customer skips a delivery | 1 |
| 3 | Win-back | no order for 60 days | 2 |

## Plans

Greenfold's "Unlimited Veg" plan lets customers skip any number of boxes. Our flows treat this plan like any other tier.

## Costs

The email platform plan (Mailer tier, around 60 USD per month for 5,000 contacts) is taken out in Greenfold's name and paid by Greenfold. SMS reminders are out of scope for now.

## Writing and changes

Copy for all 6 emails is written by us, with two rounds of edits each. After that, edits are 45 USD each email.

## Sign-off

Greenfold's marketing lead reviews the flows in the platform's preview mode within 5 working days. If we hear nothing we will send a reminder, then switch the flows on after a further 2 working days.
