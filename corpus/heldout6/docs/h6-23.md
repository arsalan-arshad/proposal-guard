# Video Production Agreement

Production company: Lumen & Loom Films
Client: Ashdown Veterinary Clinic

## Project

One 90-second promotional video about the clinic, shot in one day on site.

## Deliverables

- 1 final cut, 16:9, 4K
- 1 vertical 30-second cut for social media
- Captions in English

## Schedule

Shoot: first week of April. Rough cut: 10 working days after shoot. Final: 5 working days after feedback.

## Feedback

The client sends one consolidated list of notes on the rough cut within 5 working days. One round of edits is included. Each additional round is EUR 300 flat, agreed by e-mail before editing starts.

## Music and stock footage

Licensed music track from a library, about EUR 60, is bought by Lumen & Loom and included in the fee. Any stock footage the client asks for would be at the client's expense.

## Fee

EUR 3,200: 50% to book the shoot day, 50% on delivery of the final cut. Final cut is deemed approved if there is no reply within 5 working days.
