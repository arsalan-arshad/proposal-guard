**Sign-off:** the co-op's finance manager checks the numbers against 3 known weekly totals and replies within 5 working days. After that the dashboard is considered signed off and the last invoice is issued.

**Changes:** chart tweaks (colours, labels, ordering) are free within the first 2 weeks after delivery. New charts or new data sources: 75 GBP per hour, agreed first by e-mail.
