CONSULTING AGREEMENT

Between Aurelian Advisory (the "Consultant") and Tidewater Boat Supply Inc. (the "Company").

1. ENGAGEMENT. The Consultant will assess the Company's inventory process and deliver a written report of recommendations; the assessment will cover receiving, storage, picking and cycle counting.

2. FEE. A flat fee of CAD 9,500, invoiced as follows: CAD 3,000 on signing; CAD 3,000 on the interim findings meeting; CAD 3,500 on delivery of the final report.

3. APPROVAL. The Company's operations director will approve each deliverable. Payment of each milestone invoice falls due upon such approval.

4. SUPPORT. The Consultant will provide ongoing guidance and be available for questions throughout the implementation phase, as well as such further assistance as the Company may reasonably require.

5. TRAVEL. Travel to the Company's warehouse, including flights and hotel, shall be reimbursed at cost.

6. ADJUSTMENTS. The Consultant shall make adjustments to the report until the Company is fully satisfied.

7. GOVERNING LAW. This Agreement is governed by the laws of the Province of Ontario.
