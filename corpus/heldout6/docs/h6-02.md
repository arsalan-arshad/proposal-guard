# Master Services Agreement — Mobile Application Development

**Between:** Northgate Digital Ltd ("Developer") and Pinewood Courier Co. ("Client")

## 1. Services

1.1 The Developer shall design and build an iOS and Android application (the "App") as described in Schedule A, comprising: (a) customer login by e-mail and password; (b) parcel tracking screen showing status updates from the Client's existing API; (c) push notification opt-in screen; (d) a settings page.

1.2 The App shall load the tracking screen in under 3 seconds on a 4G connection in the test environment defined in Schedule B.

## 2. Fees and Payment

2.1 The total fee is USD 24,000, payable: 30% on signature; 40% on delivery of the beta build; 30% on store release.

2.2 Invoices are due within 14 days of issue; late sums bear interest at 1.5% per month.

## 3. Acceptance

3.1 The Client shall review each milestone deliverable within 7 business days of delivery and either accept it in writing or list the defects against Schedule A.

3.2 If no response is received within that period, the deliverable is deemed accepted.

## 4. Third-Party Costs

4.1 The Client shall pay for the Apple Developer Program and Google Play Console accounts, which shall be registered in the Client's name.

4.2 Push notification delivery charges and map-tile API usage are borne by the Client at cost, with no mark-up.

## 5. Changes

5.1 Two (2) rounds of revisions to each screen design are included in the fee.

5.2 Any change to Schedule A, or a further round, requires a written change order stating the additional fee at USD 70 per hour and any change to the delivery date.
