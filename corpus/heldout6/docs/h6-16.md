SERVICE AGREEMENT No. 2026/17

Between the Contractor, Ostrava Embedded s.r.o., and the Customer, Harbourlight Sensors Ltd.

2. Scope of Work
2.1 The Contractor shall develop firmware for the Customer's soil-moisture sensor board (Rev C) in accordance with Annex 1; the firmware shall (i) read the sensor every 15 minutes; (ii) transmit readings over LoRaWAN; (iii) enter deep sleep between readings, with average current draw not exceeding 60 µA.
2.2 The Contractor shall deliver source code and a build script in a Git repository nominated by the Customer.

3. Price and Terms of Payment
3.1 The price is EUR 11,400, payable 25% on signature, 50% on delivery of a first working build, 25% on acceptance.
3.2 The Customer shall pay each invoice within 21 days.

4. Acceptance
4.1 Acceptance tests are those listed in Annex 2. The Customer shall carry them out within 10 working days of delivery.
4.2 If the Customer does not notify defects in writing within that period, the firmware is deemed accepted.

5. Third-party Components
5.1 LoRaWAN network server subscription fees, if any, shall be borne by the Customer.
5.2 The Contractor shall use only open-source libraries under permissive licences; no licence fees arise.

6. Changes
6.1 Defects against Annex 2 are corrected free of charge. Any other change request shall be agreed in writing, with the price and delay stated, before work begins; the hourly rate is EUR 60.
