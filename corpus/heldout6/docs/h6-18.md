# Research Report Engagement

Researcher: Dr. Helena Voss
Client: SmoothPath Logistics GmbH

## Outputs

| Output | Description | Format |
|---|---|---|
| Market scan | analysis of 8 European last-mile competitors | 20 page PDF |
| Interview summary | 10 interviews with depot managers | 8 page PDF |
| Workshop | presentation of findings, 90 min | video call |

## Data sources

Paid datasets (industry database access, roughly EUR 900) are bought by the researcher and billed to the client at cost with receipts. Interview participants are paid a small thank-you of EUR 25 each; this is included in the fee.

## Review

The client reads each draft and sends consolidated comments within 10 working days. Comments arriving later may be handled in the next phase.

## Revisions

One consolidated revision per report. Anything more extensive is a new piece of work and will be priced separately.

## Fee

EUR 7,800 in three equal parts: start, draft delivery, final delivery.
