# AI Document Classifier: Pilot Proposal

Contractor: Kavya Menon
Client: Ridgeway Insurance Brokers

## Pilot goal

Sort incoming scanned documents into 6 categories (claim form, invoice, ID copy, policy schedule, letter, other).

| Metric | Target |
|---|---|
| Category accuracy on 300 labelled test documents | at least 92% |
| Time to classify one page | under 5 seconds |
| Documents that fall to "other" and need manual sorting | under 8% |

## API and compute costs

The vision-language model is called through the provider's API. Ridgeway opens the account and pays usage directly. During the pilot I estimate 150 to 250 USD in usage. Ridgeway's IT lead sets a monthly cap.

## Timeline and fee

4 weeks. Fee USD 6,000: 50% at start, 50% at the end of the pilot.

## Acceptance

Ridgeway runs the 300-document test set and replies with pass or fail within 5 working days of receiving the results report. No reply within that time means the pilot is accepted.

## Changes

One re-run after tuning is included. Adding a 7th category or another document language is a change request: written estimate first, then work.
