# Statement of Work: Mobile Banking Prototype

## 1. Scope
The prototype includes the screens listed in Schedule A. Anything not listed is out of scope.

## 2. Performance
Screens load in under 1.5 seconds on a 4G connection in at least 95% of test runs.

## 3. Acceptance
The client reviews each milestone within 7 calendar days. Milestones are deemed accepted if no written feedback is received within that period.

## 4. Revisions and changes
Each milestone includes one round of revisions. Additional revisions are billed at the rate in Schedule B and handled as change requests.

## 5. Third-party costs
The client holds the AWS and Twilio accounts and pays their fees directly.
