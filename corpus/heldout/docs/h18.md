7.1 Acceptance. Client shall accept or reject each Deliverable in writing within ten (10) business days after delivery. Any Deliverable not rejected within that period is deemed accepted.
7.2 Revisions. Contractor shall make up to two (2) revision rounds per Deliverable. Additional changes require a written change order.
